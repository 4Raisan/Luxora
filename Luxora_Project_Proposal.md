## 1. Project Title & Description

### 1.1 Project Title
**Luxora - A Subscription-Based Luxury Home Concierge Service Platform**

### 1.2 Background & Context
Modern homeowners and busy professionals often struggle to manage auto care, pet care, and garden maintenance through multiple independent service providers. Traditional booking methods are time-consuming and inconvenient, requiring customers to coordinate separately with each provider. Most existing service platforms operate only as marketplaces, leaving customers responsible for searching, comparing, and selecting providers themselves, with no ongoing service management.

Luxora is a subscription-based luxury home concierge platform developed for the Sri Lankan market. Unlike traditional marketplace platforms, Luxora delivers a managed, premium service experience by combining Auto Care, Garden Care, and Pet Care within a single platform. Customers can access services through subscription plans, combo plans, or individual, supported by KYC-verified service providers giving them a convenient, reliable, and consistently premium home service experience.

### 1.3 Purpose of the System & Main Problem
The main problem Luxora addresses is the lack of a centralized platform for managing recurring premium home services in Sri Lanka. Customers currently maintain separate relationships with different providers for auto care, gardening, and pet care, making it difficult to manage schedules, service quality, and reliability.

Luxora's purpose is to solve this by giving customers one centralized platform to subscribe to, book, and manage all three service categories. Customers can choose subscription plans, combo plans, or individual bookings based on their needs, while Luxora handles matching, scheduling, and provider verification, saving time and ensuring a consistent, reliable service experience.

### 1.4 Brief Overview of How the System Works
Customers register and log in to Luxora, browse available service categories—Auto Care, Garden Care, and Pet Care—and choose a subscription plan, combo plan, or individual booking. Once a booking is confirmed, the system assigns a suitable KYC-verified provider based on service category, customer location, date, time, and availability.

Service providers receive booking requests, manage assigned jobs, and update service status using secure PIN verification along with before-and-after service photographs. Once a service is complete, customers confirm completion, submit ratings and reviews, and can view their full service history. Administrators oversee the entire platform managing customers, providers, bookings, subscriptions, complaints, and promotions through a centralized administration dashboard.

### 1.5 Significance / Expected Impact
Luxora offers a convenient, reliable, and centralized solution for premium home service management in Sri Lanka. The expected impacts are:

* **For customers:** Customers benefit from simplified booking, flexible plans, trusted verified providers, and better time management.
* **For service providers:** Service providers gain more business opportunities, easier job management, and wider customer reach.
* **For platform operators:** Administrators gain efficient, centralized control over customers, providers, bookings, and system operations.

Overall, Luxora aims to make luxury home concierge services more convenient, reliable, and accessible, closing the coordination gap that traditional, fragmented service providers leave behind.

## 2. Objectives & Scope

### A. Objectives
* Develop a centralized, subscription-based luxury home concierge platform for the Sri Lankan market.
* Give customers convenient access to Auto Care, Garden Care, and Pet Care through a single platform.
* Allow customers to subscribe to service packages, purchase combo plans, or book individual services.
* Connect customers with KYC-verified and approved service providers.
* Simplify service booking, scheduling, and tracking end-to-end.
* Build trust through a verification and feedback system (KYC checks, before/after service photos, ratings, and reviews).
* Give administrators centralized tools to manage users, providers, services, complaints, promotions, and reports.

### B. Scope

#### I. CORE FUNCTIONALITIES (MUST-HAVE FEATURES)

**CUSTOMER MODULE:**  
The Customer module allows customers to subscribe, schedule, and manage premium home services through the Luxora platform.
* User registration and login
* Profile management
* Membership management
* Service booking & scheduling
* Secure PIN verification
* View booking status & history
* View assigned provider details
* Real-time payment gateway & Receive notifications
* Rate and review service providers

**PROVIDER MODULE:**  
The Provider module allows verified service providers to manage assigned services and maintain their availability.
* Registration & KYC verification
* Provider approval by administrators
* Login access after successful verification
* Manage service availability
* Accept assigned bookings
* Update booking status
* Start & complete services using PIN verification
* Upload before and after service completion photos
* View customer booking details
* View completed service history
* View earnings

**ADMINISTRATOR MODULE:**  
The Administrator module allows administrators to monitor and manage the overall platform.
* Central dashboard
* User management
* Provider management
* Provider approval and verification
* Subscription management
* Booking management
* Complaint handling
* Promotion management
* Customer support management
* Reports and analysis

**SERVICE CATEGORIES:**  
* **Auto Care:**
  * Wash + Vacuum
* **Garden Care:**
  * Lawn Mowing
  * Plant Watering
  * Fertilizer Application
  * Plant Health Care
  * Landscape Maintenance
* **Pet Care:**
  * Pet Bathing
  * Pet Walking
  * Pet Grooming
  * Fish Tank Cleaning
  * Water Quality Testing

#### II. OPTIONAL/ADVANCED FEATURES
* Mobile application
* Business accounts for corporate or bulk service subscriptions.
* Loyalty rewards and referral incentives.
* Additional premium service categories beyond the initial three.
* AI recommendations
* Real-time customer-provider chat
* Automated email/SMS notifications

#### III. LIMITATIONS
* Emergency Service Requests
* Customer Chooses Provider (Marketplace)
* International Operations
* Vehicle Repairs
* Veterinary Treatments
* Cash payments & Cryptocurrency Payments
* Multi-language support
